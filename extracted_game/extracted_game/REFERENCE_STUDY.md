## Visual grammar to learn (not copy)
1. Third-person elevated camera directly behind a small vehicle, with long visible road ahead and recognizable horizon perspective.
2. Stylized low-poly 3D: square buildings, textured brick or concrete, vivid cart accents, angular trees, not photorealistic visual effects.
3. Dhaka-specific signs of place: Bangla signage, dense power cables, bright footpaths and black/yellow kerbs, metro/flyover infrastructure, rikshaws and varied informal shops.
4. Atmospheric light/fog adds depth; visible traffic/pedestrian life keeps the world from feeling empty.
5. HUD floats over the world: primary money/score top left; status/mini-map top right; virtual driving controls lower corners.
6. Fast-to-understand mechanics, humorous original Bengali copy, immediate playable browser entry.

## Where THELA DHAKA should improve
- Main avatar is a **human physically pushing a three-dimensional handcart** rather than an invisible 2D sprite. The viewer sees human and vehicle together.
- Purposeful NPC system: shoppers approach the vendor, choose produce, show 5–10s sale timer, and leave; officers visibly patrol and chase.
- Clear economic model: sale value, stock, upgrades, heat/revenue tradeoffs and level-10 fixed stall.
- Clear and fair asymmetric game modes: vendor survival and police arrest career, with law enforcement as a fictional arcade story.
- Online friends match with authoritative server; no fake 1v1 label for local/shared-keyboard test.
- More effective HUD, unobtrusive controls, readable status indicators, minimized visual clutter and better 390px mobile layout.
- Original texture/model creation and game brand — avoid Baper Rasta copyright, exact art, interface layout, audio, logo and branding.

## MVP acceptance for Google AI Studio
- Actual WebGL perspective and mesh geometry, rear chase camera, vendor and cart visible together.
- One compact fictional Dhaka district, several junctions and alley escapes; recognizable street clutter and active traffic.
- Vendor role works: interact with customer, 5–10s timer, pay once, update wallet and stock, upgrades affect reward and police risk.
- Police works: chase in 3D, close-range capture, player choices affect wallet vs arrest progress.
- Responsive desktop/mobile controls and respectable frame times on target hardware.
- Test two-browser rooms only AFTER solo loop is verified; be transparent about features not finished.
